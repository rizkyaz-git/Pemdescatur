## Brand & Style

The brand personality projects institutional trust, modern administrative clarity, ecological vitality, and community empowerment. Tailored specifically for rural governance officials (Pemerintah Desa), district overseers, and public village operators, the interface strips away bureaucratic clutter in favor of an actionable, dignified, and approachable civic portal. 

The aesthetic is a union of **Modern Civic Minimalist** and **Bento Grid Architecture**:
- Structural grounding through deep civic forest greens reminiscent of agrarian landscapes, balanced by clean, highly legible clinical whites and soft sages.
- Tactile reassurance through subtle 1px border dividers, controlled ambient glows, and spacious layouts that empower village officers of varying digital literacy to navigate demographic records, land assets, public funds (APBDes), and social aid disbursements with speed and confidence.
- Emotionally, the UI evokes stability, progressive modernization without alienation, and utmost transparency.

## Colors

The palette establishes an unmistakable civic identity rooted in Indonesian agro-ecology and official governance:

- **Primary (`#0F4C3A`)**: Deep Forest Green serves as the anchor for primary actions, persistent side navigation panels, active state indicators, and authoritative headers. It conveys bureaucratic prestige and environmental alignment.
- **Secondary (`#22C55E`)**: Vibrant Leaf Green injects vitality into success alerts, growth trends, positive KPI differentials, active civic service toggles, and focal indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`)**: Amber provides high-visibility warning channels for pending social disbursements (Bansos), unresolved citizen grievances (Layanan Pengaduan), and budgetary audit notices.
- **Accent Mint (`#86EFAC`)**: Utilized primarily for subtle pill fills, highlight states, and soft badge backgrounds against dark emerald zones.
- **Dark Surface (`#072C21`)**: Emerald Dark Surface establishes dramatic contrast anchors for specialized analytical widgets, executive summary cards, and primary global navigation headers.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B`) & Slate Scales**: Deep slate for high-contrast, WCAG AA-compliant typography on white and light sage backgrounds. Mid-tone slates (`#64748B`) govern supporting metadata, table column headers, and secondary labels.
- **Canvas & Surface**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces layered cleanly atop a grounding `#F4F6F5` off-white base, segmented by razor-thin `#E2E8F0` structural boundaries.

## Typography

The type system implements a dual-typeface strategy to balance civic authority with surgical data legibility:

- **Headings & Key Metrics (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers friendly, modern, and open geometric contours with high regional relevance. All display sizes and analytical values leverage tight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to maintain tightness in expansive dashboard grids.
- **Body, Inputs & Data Tables (`Inter`)**: Deployed for its neutral, highly robust multi-screen legibility and comprehensive open-type tabular lining numerals (`tnum`).
- **Tabular Alignment Rule**: All currency displays (Rupiah values in APBDes budgets), population census metrics, NIK/ID figures, and date/time logs must activate CSS `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to guarantee vertical baseline alignment across multi-row data tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The interface relies on an asymmetric **Bento Grid** architecture nested inside a 12-column responsive fluid grid framework:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns, 24px (1.5rem) gutters, 32px (2rem) screen edge margins. Sidebar navigation spans a fixed 260px width, while analytical bento modules span contextual 3-col, 4-col, 6-col, 8-col, or 12-col spans.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, 16px (1rem) gutters, 24px (1.5rem) margins. The navigation drawer collapses into a responsive 72px rail or an off-canvas drawer. Bento layouts simplify into 2-column or full-width patterns.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns, 12px (0.75rem) gutters, 16px (1rem) margins. Layout cascades linearly into a single vertical stream. Touch targets maintain a minimum vertical clearance of 48px.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Constructed strictly on an 8pt baseline (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`), using a secondary 4pt half-step exclusively for micro-component internal padding and metadata tag offsets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses clean **Layered Structural Elevation** rather than deep, heavy shadows, maintaining crisp contrast against rural sunlight environments:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#F4F6F5` soft off-white, completely flat.
- **Level 1 (Bento Surface)**: `#FFFFFF` pure white, bound by a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` border and an ambient shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 76, 58, 0.04), 0px 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`.
- **Level 1 Highlight (Dark Bento Card)**: `#072C21` with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` border and `0px 8px 24px rgba(7, 44, 33, 0.18)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Flyouts)**: Elevated state when interacting with dashboard cards or data rows: `0px 8px 20px -2px rgba(15, 76, 58, 0.08), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`. Card translates vertically by `-2px` smoothly (`transition: all 200ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Dropdowns)**: Floating system overlays, action menus, and citizen registration drawers: `0px 16px 32px -4px rgba(15, 76, 58, 0.12), 0px 6px 12px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)` with a backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) over a 40% `#072C21` scrim.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on an approachable yet disciplined shape hierarchy:

- **Bento Dashboard Cards**: Standardized to `20px` border radius (`rounded-2xl` / custom `1.25rem`), generating friendly containment for high-density administrative widgets.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Base Buttons)**: Standardized to `10px` or `12px` border radius (`rounded-lg`), presenting crisp, precise tactile targets.
- **Status Badges, Indicators & Pills**: Strictly circular/pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / `9999px`), ensuring immediate cognitive distinction between rectangular data cards and interactive or informational status chips.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Civic Action**: Filled with Primary Deep Forest Green (`#0F4C3A`), pure white text, semi-bold font weight, 10px corner radius, padding 10px 18px. Hover shifts to `#165A46` with Level 2 elevation.
- **Secondary Action**: White surface, 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), neutral slate text (`#1E293B`). Hover shifts to `#F8FAFC` canvas with `#0F4C3A` border tint.
- **Destructive**: Soft red wash surface (`#FEF2F2`), border (`#FCA5A5`), bold red text (`#DC2626`).

### 2. Status Chips & Badges
- Strictly pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `font-size: 11px`, `font-weight: 600`, padding `4px 10px`.
- **Approved / Active (Disalurkan / Selesai)**: Mint tint (`#DCFCE7`) background with `#15803D` green text. Includes a 6px circular green pulse dot.
- **Pending Review (Menunggu Verifikasi)**: Amber wash (`#FEF3C7`) background with `#B45309` amber text.
- **Rejected / Urgent**: Red wash (`#FEE2E2`) background with `#B91C1C` text.

### 3. Bento Grid Cards
- Background: `#FFFFFF` or `#072C21` (High-tier KPIs).
- Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Radius: `20px`. Padding: `24px`.
- Header: Upper-left typography grouping paired with an optional top-right contextual action menu (icon trigger) or period toggle.
- Hover Effect: Subtle border color transition to `#CBD5E1` and `-2px` vertical translation.

### 4. Data Tables (Sistem Kependudukan & Transaksi Desa)
- Header: `#F8FAFC` background, 11px uppercase slate text (`#64748B`), tracking `0.05em`, border bottom `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- Rows: `#FFFFFF` base, height 52px, horizontal border bottom `1px solid #F1F5F9`. Hover state activates `#F8FAF9` across the row.
- Numbers/IDs: Tabular numbers applied, aligned right for financials and population metrics.

### 5. Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background: `#FFFFFF`, height 42px, radius 10px, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`.
- Focus State: Border color moves to `#0F4C3A` accompanied by an accessible 3px outer ring in `rgba(15, 76, 58, 0.12)`.
- Global Search includes a built-in shortcut indicator badge (`Ctrl + K` or `⌘K`) in muted slate.

### 6. Village Domain Specific Components
- **APBDes Budget Progress Bar**: Dual-toned segmented bar (`#22C55E` executed, `#E2E8F0` remaining allocation) with integrated percentage pill badge.
- **Citizen Verification Card**: Compact bento widget showcasing citizen avatar/NIK photo alongside fast-action dual approval toggles (`Terima` / `Tolak`).
